## 9. Timing modes

Isolated (events around one launch on an idle GPU: the verdict measure), spin (the same with a spin kernel before the start event: a diagnostic, see `results/timing_method/`) and streamed (whole rotation cycles in one CUDA graph) for the ladder kernels, all matrices.

- streamed / isolated: median 0.970, range 0.848–1.003; for kernels under 30 µs: median 0.918; over 1 ms: 1.000. Isolated − streamed: median 3.17 µs; part of that is the event overhead that the streamed mode does not pay per launch (final-run events exceed the Nsight kernel durations by a median 2.4 µs, IQR 1.7–2.9), the rest is launch start-up and tail that back-to-back kernels overlap.
- spin − isolated: median +0.34 µs (range -3.54 to +5.55); negative mostly for cuBLAS, whose plain-event time includes part of its host-side call path (≤ 1% on the H1/H2 matrices, 1.6–1.8% on the 21 MB ones).
- Residual caching: 158 of 11000 isolated samples of the ladder kernels in execution 1 are ≥ 15% faster than their row's median, in 25 of 55 rows, about one per rotation cycle (e.g. `down` v2: 11 of 200 with R = 18) — one copy of the rotation evidently still meets a warm cache or a favourable placement. The median ignores them; streamed means include them (≲ 1%).

## 19. End-to-end decode in vLLM (open item 8.4)

`results/e2e/` (`scripts/run_stage.sh e2e`): vLLM 0.28.0 (NVIDIA GB10), batch 1, greedy, prefix caching off, CUDA graphs on, prompt = the first P tokens of the WikiText-2 test text, 256 forced decode steps; decode tokens/s = 256 / (median t(1 + 256 tokens) − median t(1 token)), so prefill and per-request costs cancel; 3 executions (separate processes, formats in shuffled order), median shown with the range. The INT8/INT4 checkpoints are written by `scripts/make_quant_checkpoint.py` from this project's quantizer in GPTQ format (served by vLLM's Marlin kernels); every large layer matrix is quantized (200 matrices; the codes and scales of the ten benchmarked layer matrices are bit-identical to the kernels' operands, checked twice — by the writer and by an independent read-back of the files; vLLM then stores the FP16 scales in BF16, a rounding of at most 2⁻⁹ per scale), the tied embedding/LM head and the small tensors stay BF16. Perplexity: WikiText-2 test, 8 windows of 1024 tokens. `GEMV model`: the sum, over one token's large GEMVs, of the streamed times measured in this project (§12) — cuBLAS BF16 for the BF16 model and the LM head, Marlin (vLLM 0.16, §7) for the INT4 layers, and my v3 as a stand-in for the INT8 layers (no Marlin INT8 timing exists); vLLM also fuses gate/up and q/k/v into single GEMVs, so it is a model, not a measurement.
